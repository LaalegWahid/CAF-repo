# CAF prototype design audit — 2026-10-01

## Surface audited

Current mobile opening view of the local prototype at `http://localhost:5173/`.

Evidence: `qa/implementation-mobile.png`.

## Intended product

The prototype was built as a focused acquisition microsite for France-based prospects and members of the Moroccan diaspora who want to create or operate a company in Morocco. It was not intended to be CAF Management's main corporate website.

## Verdict

The page is usable and visually coherent, but the purpose is not explicit enough and the art direction reads as a polished agency template rather than discreet, high-end professional advisory.

## Steps and health

1. Brand/header — Needs redesign. The large white header, small logo, and boxed menu control do not create a premium first impression.
2. Positioning/hero — Needs redesign. “Votre projet au Maroc” is broad; the exact offer and target audience are not immediately named.
3. Primary action — Functional but generic. The full-width cyan rectangle resembles a standard SaaS or agency conversion pattern.
4. Visual language — Needs redesign. Uppercase geometric sans-serif, numbered boxes, repeated borders, and cyan rules are consistent but familiar and over-structured.
5. Trust — Underused above the fold. CAF's 1993 history, real team, multilingual capability, and international expertise should do more of the luxury work than campaign styling.

## Highest-impact changes

- Make the offer explicit: “Créer votre entreprise au Maroc, depuis la France.”
- Decide whether this is the acquisition microsite or CAF's corporate website before redesigning the information architecture.
- Replace the single-font uppercase system with a distinctive editorial display face and a restrained text face.
- Shift from navy/cyan/white to near-black navy, warm ivory, stone, and limited cyan accents.
- Remove the six-card service grid and most borders. Use fewer, larger narrative sections.
- Eliminate generic icons and decorative animation; rely on typography, spacing, real evidence, and photography.
- Keep AI imagery atmospheric and clearly separate from real CAF people, offices, and proof.

## Evidence limits

This audit covers the visible opening state and its information hierarchy. It does not claim full accessibility compliance or validate the final information architecture, which depends on confirming the website's role.
